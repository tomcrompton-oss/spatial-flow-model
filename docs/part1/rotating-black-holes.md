# Rotating Black Holes

Round a body that doesn't spin, space only ever flows straight towards it. Round a spinning body it turns as it
falls: in the river model of a rotating (Kerr) black hole the river flows straight in but also *twists*, and that
twist is what drags frames round the hole. The simulation includes it (`engine/kerr.py`), and matches exact Kerr for
light bending and orbits in the equatorial plane.
