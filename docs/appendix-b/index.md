# Appendix B – Rabbit Holes

!!! note "Still to be written"
    This appendix's introduction will be written last, with the Synopsis, the Preface and the Conclusion, once the
    rest of the document is final. The pages below are first drafts: each records a side path the model took, what
    was found, and why it isn't on the main path.

Not every idea tried on the way to the model made it into the main text. Some failed in instructive ways; some
answered a question the main text doesn't need to ask. They are collected here.

- **[How Much Gravity Can Go in the Clocks?](clocks.md)** The flow model puts all of gravity into the flow. A whole
  family of models moves part of it into the clocks instead, and every member is general relativity.
- **[Each Body Separately](each-body-separately.md)** Give each body its own exact one-body flow and add their effects.
  It passes most tests with one body much heavier, and fails binaries of comparable masses.
- **[Two Checks on Every Model](two-checks.md)** Does a model keep the energy its symmetries require, and does its
  space fall at Newton's $g$? The two checks pull opposite ways.
- **[Flows With Curl](flows-with-curl.md)** The other way out of Part 3's problem of one constant for every body, and
  why Einstein's equations close it.
- **[One Time or Many](one-time-or-many.md)** What goes wrong at a moving seam when the two sides' times don't agree.
- **[The Step and the Geodesic](step-and-geodesic.md)** Where the flow has curl, Part 1's procedure and the true path
  through flowing space part company.
