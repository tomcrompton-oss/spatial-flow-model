# Conclusion

## Where we started

Part 2 ended with a flow that is general relativity for any number of bodies at rest: space flowing with no swirl, at
the escape speed from all the bodies together, and stretched across the flow by $u^2/c^2$. Part 3 set the bodies
moving, on paths that were given, and asked what flow a moving body carries.

## What we built

[Carrying the Flow](carrying-the-flow.md) found that a body carries its space, not just the pattern of its flow: the
space round the moving Earth moves at the Earth's velocity plus its fall towards it. Taking only the pattern along
fails Newton's gravity at once. For the Sun and the Earth going round each other, the potential flow can be built in
the frame turning with them: the turning flow. Its paths share one constant, chosen so that the Earth's flow is its
own, and it passes Newton's gravity round the moving Earth and lunar laser ranging's Sun term.

[Curving the Turning Flow](curving-the-turning-flow.md) showed the cost of that constant: it squeezes the Sun's space, and light
grazing the Sun is bent 9.6 mas too little. Curving space across the flow, as in Part 2, fixed it, because light sees
only the flow's speed.

[One Potential](one-potential.md) found where the constant can't be hidden: the Moon's de Sitter turn, and light
past a moving Jupiter. Setting it to zero, as in Parts 1 and 2, gives one potential for the whole plane, keeping
Bernoulli's equation with no constant added. With space curved across it, and the small corrections to the flow that
general relativity gives with the bodies moving, it is general relativity in the time of clocks falling with space.

Only one potential passes every test it was run on, and, like curved space in Part 2, it does so because it is general
relativity, re-timed by clocks falling with space. Its Moon matches general relativity's to the method's floor, a few
centimetres over 90 days.

## What is left

Every body in Part 3 was given its path: the Earth on a circle round the Sun, Jupiter on its orbit. Only the Moon, too
light to matter to the others, moved by the flow. In a real pair of stars, each moves by the other's flow, and the
paths are the answer, not the question. [Part 4](../part4/bodies-moved-by-the-flow.md) lets the bodies move by the flow, and asks whether
their orbits, and the pair as a whole, come out as general relativity says.
